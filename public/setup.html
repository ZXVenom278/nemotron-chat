Set up Nemotron

A free AI chat (NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra). Takes about 5 minutes. Follow the steps in order.

🔒 Private: your chats and your key are saved only on your own device. The person who made this site cannot see them.

Part 1: Get your free key (do this once)

The "key" is a long password-like code from NVIDIA that lets the app talk to the AI. It is free.

  1. Open this link: build.nvidia.com/nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b
  2. Tap Login (top right). Type your email and tap Next. No NVIDIA account? It will offer to make one. Just follow along and confirm the email it sends you.
  3. Once you're signed in, you're back on the Nemotron page. Tap Get API Key (or View Code → Get API Key).
  4. Tap Generate Key. If it asks for a name, type nemotron. Leave the rest as is.
  5. A long code shows up that starts with nvapi-. Tap Copy. ⚠ NVIDIA shows it only once. Paste it into your Notes app right now so you don't lose it.

Part 2: Put Nemotron on your device

⬇ Download Nemotron for PC
  1. Click the green Download Nemotron for PC button above.
  2. Your browser might warn you about the file. Click Keep (in Edge you may have to click … first, then Keep, then Keep anyway).
  3. Open the file Nemotron-Setup.bat (click it at the bottom or top right of your browser, or find it in your Downloads folder).
  4. If a blue box says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. Windows shows that box for any file it doesn't know. This file only puts a Nemotron icon on your desktop.
  5. A black window shows up for a few seconds, then closes. You now have a Nemotron icon on your desktop and in the Start menu, and the app opens.
  6. The app asks for your key. Paste it (Ctrl + V) and click Save.
✅ Done. To use it later, double-click the Nemotron icon on your desktop.

Download didn't work? Open nemotron-ultra-chat.netlify.app in Edge or Chrome and click the install icon (a small screen with a ⬇ arrow) at the right end of the address bar, then Install.

  1. On your iPhone, open Safari (the blue compass). Other browsers can't do this step.
  2. Go to nemotron-ultra-chat.netlify.app.
  3. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up). It's at the bottom of the screen, or tap … first on newer iPhones.
  4. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add (top right).
  5. Go to your Home Screen and tap the new green N icon called Nemotron.
  6. It asks for your key. Tap the box, tap Paste, then tap Save.
✅ Done. To use it later, just tap the Nemotron icon.

Want your own copy of this site? (optional)

You don't need this to use the chat. It gives you your own site on your own free Netlify account, so it's fully yours.

🚀 Own this site
  1. Click the green Own this site button above.
  2. Click Connect to GitHub and sign in. No GitHub account? Click Create an account, it's free.
  3. If it asks to create a Netlify account, click Sign up with GitHub and Authorize.
  4. Leave everything as it is and click Save & Deploy (or Deploy).
  5. Wait about a minute until it says Published. Your site's link is shown at the top (it ends in .netlify.app).
  6. Open your new link and add /setup.html to the end. Do Part 2 from there so the app uses your site.

Tip: in Netlify, Project configuration → Change project name gives your link a nicer name.

Using it

  1. Type a message at the bottom and press Send.
  2. Want better answers to hard questions? Turn on Think. It's slower but smarter.
  3. New starts a fresh chat. ⚙ lets you change your key.

If something goes wrong

  1. "key is wrong" → tap ⚙ and paste your key again. Make sure it starts with nvapi- and has no spaces.
  2. Lost your key → do Part 1 again to make a new one.
  3. Nothing happens / error 429 → wait a minute and try again. The free key has a limit on how fast you can send messages.
