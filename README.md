# Nemotron Chat

A private chat app for NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra. Works on iPhone, Android and PC.
Each person uses their own free NVIDIA key, which stays on their own device.

## Get your own copy

[![Deploy to Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/img/deploy/button.svg)](https://app.netlify.com/start/deploy?repository=https://github.com/ZXVenom278/nemotron-chat)

Click the button, sign in, then click **Save & Deploy**. Your copy goes live in about a minute.
